03 / Thinking

Product questions came first.

Hover a question.

Hover a question
Who is this for? People new in the city — or restarting their social life. Not dating.
Why join? A concrete plan this week — not a maybe calendar.
What lowers anxiety? Come for the activity. Guest list private until after. ~8 people.
What does a host need? Fill seats, verify payments, run a series without chaos.
What brings people back? Familiar faces. Chat after. Next week’s drop.
How do they pay? InstaPay host↔attendee. GatherUp isn’t merchant of record.
