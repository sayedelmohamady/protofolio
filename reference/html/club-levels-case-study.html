01 Overview

A progression system for
ambassador engagement.

Club Levels adds tiers, points, and unlocks to the Club ambassador experience so members can see where they stand and what comes next. Status and perks scale with real participation—linking quick wins to sustained advocacy—on iOS and Android.

03 The Problem

Engagement had no engine. Users acted once — then disappeared.

No progress. No status. No reason to stay.

Inconsistent engagement

Spikes at launch, then drops. Nothing to sustain participation.

No sense of progress

No visibility into standing, distance covered, or what to work toward.

Post-mission drop-off

First mission done — then gone. No pull to come back.

No segmentation for brands

Everyone looked the same. No way to identify or reward top performers.

04 Goal

Give participation
a direction.

Design a system that makes long-term engagement feel worthwhile — for ambassadors and the brands who need to identify their best ones.

Encourage continuous engagement

A reason to return — not just when missions drop, but as a habit.

Create a visible sense of progress

Make it clear where each ambassador stands, how far they've come, and what they're working toward next.

Help brands identify top users

A clear signal of who's most engaged — so brands can treat them differently.

Keep the system simple

Earn points, level up, unlock more. No confusion.

05 Solution

Introducing Levels.

Mission activity converts into visible status. Points unlock levels. Levels unlock real perks — higher commissions, early access, brand recognition. Every reward is tangible, not just a badge.

06 How It Works

Four steps.
One loop.

The system is built around a simple, repeatable loop. Each step is visible, each reward is tangible, and each level-up moment is designed to feel earned — not automatic.

09 UX Decisions

The thinking behind
the key calls.

Three principles behind the most important calls in Levels.

A

Clarity of Progress

If people can’t see where they stand, motivation drops. Every screen shows level, points to the next tier, and what unlocks next—no hunting. The progress bar stays visible and honest.

Information Design
B

Avoiding Gamification Noise

No streak walls or empty badges. Each tier ties to real upside—commissions, early access, brand collaboration—so trust holds after the first week.

Product Strategy
C

Motivation Design

Level-up is the emotional peak: full-screen, not a toast. Perks reveal in steps so the moment feels earned and easy to remember.

Interaction Design

11 Learnings

What this project
taught me.

  • 01 Progression needs an anchor in reality.

    Tie levels to real perks—not badges. Concrete value beats abstract gamification.

  • 02 Emotional moments need dedicated space.

    Level-up is full-screen on purpose. People remember moments, not flows.

  • 03 Simplicity is a feature.

    Four clear tiers beat endless noise. Legible progress feels achievable.

  • 04 Progress visibility drives retention.

    The “64% to Gold” bar brought people back—the gap creates pull.

  • 05 Design for both sides.

    Ambassadors see status; brands see top 10%. Neither is an afterthought.

Closing thought
When progression works, it feels invisible—people just keep showing up.
Engagement lasts when every step maps to value outside the app. Badges without perks are noise.
