01 Overview

A Gamification
system for
ambassador

Ambassadors stick around when effort feels like it adds up—not when every mission resets the story to zero. Club Levels is a progression layer in the Club app: tiers, points, and unlockable perks that make status and next steps legible on iOS and Android, while giving brands a fairer read on who is actually driving engagement over time.

02 The Problem

Engagement had no engine. Users acted once — then disappeared.

No progress. No status. No reason to stay.

Inconsistent engagement

Spikes at launch, then drops. Nothing to sustain participation.

No sense of progress

No visibility into standing, distance covered, or what to work toward.

Post-mission drop-off

First mission done — then gone. No pull to come back.

No segmentation for brands

Everyone looked the same. No way to identify or reward top performers.

03 Solution

Introducing Levels.

Mission activity converts into visible status. Points unlock levels. Levels unlock real perks — higher commissions, early access, brand recognition. Every reward is tangible, not just a badge.

04 How It Works

Four steps.
One loop.

The system is built around a simple, repeatable loop. Each step is visible, each reward is tangible, and each level-up moment is designed to feel earned — not automatic.

Complete missions
Take real-world actions for brands
Earn points
Points reflect engagement quality
Level up
Reach thresholds to unlock next tier
Unlock rewards
Real perks tied to each new level

Hover or focus a step to see how it connects in the loop.

07 UX Decisions

The thinking behind
the key calls.

Three principles for the strongest calls in Levels.

A

Clarity of Progress

Level, distance to next tier, and next unlock—always in view. No hunting; the bar stays honest.

Information Design
B

Avoiding Gamification Noise

No empty badges. Tiers map to commissions, access, and collaboration—trust has to last past week one.

Product Strategy
C

Motivation Design

Level-up is full-screen, not a toast. Perks land in steps so the win feels earned and memorable.

Interaction Design
08 Learnings

What this project
taught me.

  • 01 Progression needs an anchor in reality.

    Perks, not badges. Real value beats abstract gamification.

  • 02 Emotional moments need dedicated space.

    Full-screen level-ups on purpose—people remember moments, not flows.

  • 03 Simplicity is a feature.

    Four tiers beat noise. Legible progress feels doable.

  • 04 Progress visibility drives retention.

    A visible “64% to Gold” bar brings people back.

  • 05 Design for both sides.

    Status for ambassadors; top 10% for brands—both first-class.

Closing thought
When progression works, it feels invisible—people just keep showing up.
Engagement lasts when steps map to value outside the app.
